Pressurized Tank with Suspended Load: FEM Analysis in ANSYS

SolidWorks · ANSYS Mechanical · Static structural analysis

Project Description

This project is a finite element analysis of a steel pressurized tank subjected to an internal pressure of 1 MPa and to the weight of a 200 kg pump-motor assembly mounted on the top nozzle.

The objective is to evaluate the structural response of the tank, in particular its deformation and von Mises stress, and to study how the numerical results behave in the region where the highest stresses are expected.

The work is carried out in four steps, from the geometry design in SolidWorks to the analysis of the results and the adaptive mesh refinement.

Study at a Glance

  • Software: SolidWorks, ANSYS Mechanical
  • Analysis type: Static structural
  • Internal pressure: 1 MPa
  • Suspended load: 200 kg point mass on the top nozzle
  • Material: Structural steel

Part 1 — Geometry Design in SolidWorks

The tank geometry was created in SolidWorks. The model includes the main cylindrical vessel, its supports, the end caps and the top nozzle used to connect the pump-motor assembly.

The geometry was prepared with the finite element analysis in mind, while keeping the structural features that may influence the stress distribution.

Part 2 — Geometry Preparation in ANSYS

The SolidWorks geometry was imported into ANSYS and divided into two regions.

This split allows a more controlled treatment of the area where the highest stresses are expected, since a localized mesh refinement can be applied there if needed. It also avoids refining the entire tank when a local refinement is sufficient.

Part 3 — Material, Boundary Conditions and Loading

The model was set up in ANSYS Mechanical for a static structural analysis, with steel as the material.

Boundary Conditions and Loads

The tank was constrained at its supports. The loads were introduced through load steps so that the different actions on the structure are applied progressively:

  • Gravity accounts for the weight of the structure.
  • Internal pressure of 1 MPa is applied to the internal surfaces of the tank. A cross-section view was used to select these surfaces easily.
  • Pump-motor assembly: a 200 kg point mass located at the top nozzle. This includes the effect of the suspended equipment without modelling the whole assembly.

Part 4 — Results and Adaptive Mesh Refinement

After solving the model, the main results were examined through the total deformation and von Mises stress distributions.

The maximum von Mises stress of 61.1 MPa is well below the 250 MPa tensile yield strength of the steel, which gives a margin of about 4 on yielding in this initial solution.

Adaptive Mesh Refinement

An adaptive mesh refinement study is then performed to check how the mesh influences the numerical results, particularly in the region where the highest stresses occur.

The refined results are compared with the initial solution to assess the convergence of the calculated stress and deformation.

Conclusion

The initial simulation gives a first assessment of the structural behavior of the pressurized tank under internal pressure, gravity and the additional load of the 200 kg pump-motor assembly.

The maximum von Mises stress is 61.1 MPa and the maximum total deformation is 0.05 mm. The stress remains below the 250 MPa yield strength of the material.

The adaptive mesh refinement study is used to confirm the reliability and convergence of these results.
